A Car Wash in a Bottle.

Sproozt is a waterless car wash spray. Spray it on, wipe it off with a microfibre cloth, and your car is clean — no hose, no bucket, no water.

  • No Water Needed

    The spray does the work a hose and bucket normally do, so you can clean your car without a water source.

  • Cleans, Polishes and Protects

    The formula lifts dirt away from the surface so it can be wiped off gently, and leaves a protective layer behind — all in one step.

  • Safe on Every Exterior Surface

    Paint, glass, chrome, alloy wheels and plastic trim. No abrasives, acids or solvents.

  • Use It Anywhere

    Driveway, garage or parking space. All you need is the bottle and a clean cloth.

1

Spray

Apply Sproozt directly onto one section of your car at a time.

2

Wipe

Glide a clean microfibre cloth across the surface to lift the dirt away.

3

Shine

Buff with a dry side of the cloth, then move on to the next section.
