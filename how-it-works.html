The Process

Three Steps to a
Perfect Finish.

No equipment to set up. No water source needed. No specialist knowledge required. Just three effortless steps to a result you'd expect from a professional detailer.

01

Spray

Apply Sproozt directly to a section of your car's exterior. The formula instantly begins lifting and encapsulating dirt, dust, and contaminants at a molecular level — safely suspending them away from the paintwork.

02

Wipe

Using a clean microfibre cloth, glide across the surface in smooth, even strokes. The lubricating formula ensures zero scratching — even on sensitive, freshly painted surfaces. Work in small sections for the best result.

03

Reveal

Buff to a deep, mirror-like shine. Your vehicle is clean, polished, and polymer-protected — with no water, no bucket, and no mess left behind. Repeat across the full vehicle in minutes.

Pro Tips

Get the Most
From Every Spray

🧴

Work in Sections

Divide your car into panels and treat one at a time. This prevents the formula from drying before you wipe, ensuring a streak-free result every time.

🧽

Use a Clean Cloth

Always use a fresh, clean microfibre cloth. A dirty cloth can redistribute contaminants. Fold your cloth regularly to always have a clean face to wipe with.

🌤️

Avoid Direct Sunlight

For optimal results, apply in shade or during cooler parts of the day. This gives the formula more time to work before it evaporates, for a deeper clean.
