1About this policy

Sproozt has not launched yet. Right now, this website has one purpose: letting people join the waitlist so we can tell them when Sproozt launches.

This policy explains what happens to the information you give us when you join the waitlist. It does not cover any shop, purchases or customer accounts — those don’t exist yet. When Sproozt launches, we will publish an updated policy and let waitlist members know by email.

2Who we are

Sproozt AB is responsible for your personal data (the “data controller”). We handle it in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Swedish data protection law.

Company
Sproozt

3What we collect

To join the waitlist (required)

  • First name
  • Email address

If you choose to tell us (optional)

  • Last name and phone number
  • Age range — we never ask for your date of birth
  • Gender, or your own description if you choose “Self-describe”
  • The type of car you drive and how often you wash it
  • Whether you want to receive Sproozt news and offers

What we add

  • Your waitlist member number (for example SPZ-00042) and the date you joined

Like any website, our hosting provider also processes basic technical data when you visit — such as your IP address and browser type — to deliver the page and protect it against abuse.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.

5Optional questions

You can join the waitlist without answering any of the optional questions, and skipping them doesn’t affect your place on the list.

If you do answer, we only use your answers as combined statistics — for example, how many people on the waitlist drive an SUV — to help us decide what to build. We don’t use them to target you individually.

If you choose “Self-describe” for gender, please don’t include other sensitive information, such as details about your health.

6Launch emails and updates

By joining the waitlist, you’ll get emails about your place on the list and about the launch itself — that’s what the waitlist is for.

Other news and offers are separate: we only send those if you tick the box in the form. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. If you unsubscribe from news and offers, you stay on the waitlist.

7Who handles your data

We use a few service providers to run the waitlist. They only process your data on our behalf and according to our instructions:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS), France — stores the waitlist and sends our emails.
  • Netlify, Inc., USA — hosts this website and receives your sign-up.
  • Google Fonts (Google) — supplies the website’s typefaces. When the page loads, Google receives your IP address.

Netlify and Google may process data in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (where the provider is certified) or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

We don’t share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

8How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist details until you leave the waitlist, and no longer than [12 months] after Sproozt launches. If Sproozt doesn’t launch, we delete the waitlist.

If you unsubscribe from news and offers, we keep a note of that so we don’t contact you again. Combined statistics that can’t identify you may be kept after your details are deleted.

9Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Leave the waitlist and have your details deleted
  • See the data we hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • Correct anything that’s wrong
  • Withdraw your consent — for example, to your optional answers or to news and offers
  • Object to or restrict how we use your data

Just email [privacy contact email]. We’ll reply within one month, and may ask you to confirm it’s really you first. Withdrawing consent doesn’t affect how we used your data before you withdrew it.

10Cookies, security and age limit

Cookies. This website doesn’t use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking.

Security. Your sign-up is sent over an encrypted connection, the keys to the waitlist are kept secret on the server, and only people who need it can access the list.

Age limit. The waitlist is for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we’ll delete their details.

11Contact and complaints

Questions about this policy or your data? Email [privacy contact email].

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY) at www.imy.se, or to the data protection authority where you live.
